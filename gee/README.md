# Google Earth Engine Application

<div align="justify">

The interactive Google Earth Engine application supports satellite-based exploration and monitoring of hazelnut orchards in Sakarya Province.

</div>

**Launch the application:** [Sakarya Hazelnut Frost Monitoring](https://ee-smhsng2.projects.earthengine.app/view/sakarya-hazelnut-frost-monitoring)

<div align="justify">

Implemented research capabilities include:

</div>

- selection of frost-affected orchard parcels;
- custom point, rectangle, and polygon analysis;
- RGB, false-color, NDVI, SAVI, and EVI visualization;
- NDVI, SAVI, and EVI time-series analysis;
- linked split-map comparison of 2024 and 2025 observations;
- phenological monitoring;
- comparison of observations associated with spring frost;
- parcel-level exploration.

## Release status

<div align="justify">

The public application is deployed and available through the link above. Its source is maintained in Google Earth Engine and is not distributed through this repository. The standalone [six-date Sentinel-2 Drive export script](../scripts/preprocessing/export_sentinel2_six_dates.js) remains available for reproducible imagery export.
